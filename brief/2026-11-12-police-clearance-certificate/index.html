Need a police clearance certificate? How UAE residents can get one

Employers, embassies and immigration offices abroad often ask for a police clearance (good conduct) certificate. UAE residents can request one from the UAE police online.

What it is

A police clearance certificate, also called a good conduct certificate, confirms whether a person has a criminal record in the UAE. It is issued by the UAE Ministry of Interior and the police of each emirate, such as Dubai Police.

It is often needed for a new job, a long-term visa in another country, or studying abroad. Some countries also ask for it to be attested.

Who it affects

Residents and former residents applying for jobs, immigration or study, in the UAE or abroad.

What to do

  • Check whether the requester needs the certificate from the federal Ministry of Interior or from a specific emirate.
  • Ask if it must be attested or translated.
  • Send us your passport, Emirates ID and the purpose on WhatsApp. We apply and arrange attestation if needed.
