Hiring a new employee? The work permit and visa steps in order

For mainland companies, a new hire usually goes through MOHRE approval, an entry permit, a medical test, Emirates ID and the residence visa. Starting in the right order saves weeks.

What it is

Mainland companies hire foreign staff through the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE). The company applies for a work permit, the employee signs the employment contract, and the immigration steps follow: entry permit or status change, medical test, Emirates ID and residence visa.

Free zone companies follow a similar path through their free zone authority instead of MOHRE.

Who it affects

Business owners, HR staff and PRO officers hiring new employees, and employees joining a new company.

What to do

  • Check the company's establishment card and licence are valid before applying.
  • Collect the employee's passport, photo and, where the job needs it, the attested degree.
  • Agree the job title and salary first, because they appear on the contract.
  • Our PRO team runs every step and tells you what is next at each stage.
