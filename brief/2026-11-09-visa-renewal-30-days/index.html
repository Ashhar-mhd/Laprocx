Residence visa expiring? Start the renewal 30 days before

Renewal has several steps, and each one needs a slot. Starting a month early means no rush and no late fines.

What it is

Renewing a residence visa is not one step. It usually needs a medical fitness test, Emirates ID biometrics, valid health insurance and the visa application itself.

Each step can take a few days, and appointment slots fill up quickly before holidays and school terms.

Who it affects

Anyone whose residence visa ends in the next 60 days, and sponsors renewing visas for family members or staff.

What to do

  • Check your exact expiry date in the ICP app, or the GDRFA app if your visa is from Dubai.
  • Make sure your passport has enough validity left. If it is close to expiry, renew it first.
  • Check that your health insurance covers the new visa period.
  • Send us your passport, visa page, Emirates ID and a photo on WhatsApp. We reply with the full document list and the fees.
