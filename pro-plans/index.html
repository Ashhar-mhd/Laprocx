Common questions

What counts as one transaction?

One application to one government office, for example one visa renewal or one licence renewal. We list them on your monthly report.

Are government fees included?

No. Government fees are charged at cost and shown separately on every invoice.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. You can move between plans or stop at the end of any month.
