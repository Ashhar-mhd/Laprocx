A 10-year renewable residence visa that does not need an employer as sponsor. We tell you which category fits and prepare your file.

Who it's for

Property investors, skilled professionals, entrepreneurs, outstanding students and other eligible categories.

Documents you usually need

  • Passport copy and photo
  • Proof for your category: title deed, salary certificate and attested degree, or company documents
  • Current visa and Emirates ID, if in the UAE
  • Health insurance

How it works

  1. Eligibility check on WhatsApp
  2. Nomination or approval for your category
  3. Entry permit or change of status
  4. Medical test and Emirates ID biometrics
  5. Golden visa issued

Fees

You pay two things: the government fees, charged at cost, and our service fee. We send both in a written quote before we start, so there are no surprises.

Common questions

What are the main ways to qualify?

Common routes include property worth at least AED 2 million, a monthly salary of AED 30,000 or more with a degree for skilled professionals, approved entrepreneurs, and outstanding students. Each has detailed conditions on the UAE government portal.

Can I sponsor my family?

Golden visa holders can usually sponsor their spouse and children. We add them to your application plan.
