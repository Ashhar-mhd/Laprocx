Common questions

Before you ask

Do I need to visit your office?

Usually not. Most applications start with photos of your documents on WhatsApp. We only ask for originals when the government office requires them.

Are you a government service centre?

No. Laprocx is a private business services company. We prepare your applications, submit them through the official channels and follow up until they're approved.

How do I know the fees upfront?

We send a written quote that separates government fees from our service fee before we start.

Which languages do you speak?

Arabic and English, plus other languages within the team.
