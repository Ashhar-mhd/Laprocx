Investor and partner visa

If you own or co-own a UAE company, you can sponsor your own residence visa through it. We prepare and follow the full application.

Who it's for

Owners and partners named on a UAE trade licence.

Documents you usually need

  • Passport copy and photo
  • Trade licence showing you as owner or partner
  • Memorandum of association or share certificate
  • Establishment card

We confirm the exact list for your case on WhatsApp before you start.

How it works

  1. Check your share and eligibility
  2. Entry permit or change of status
  3. Medical test and Emirates ID biometrics
  4. Residence visa issued

Fees

You pay two things: the government fees, charged at cost, and our service fee. We send both in a written quote before we start, so there are no surprises.

Common questions

Can I sponsor my family on an investor visa?

Usually yes, once your own visa is issued and you meet the sponsor conditions. See our family visa service.

Is this the same as the Golden visa?

No. The investor visa is linked to your company. The Golden visa is a 10-year visa with its own criteria.
